Why a box truck?

The right size for the job

Many shipments get forced into the wrong mode. LTL means terminals, transfers and damage risk. A 53' trailer means paying for empty space and getting turned away at places a tractor-trailer can't reach.

  • Liftgate: deliver to locations without a loading dock
  • Fits where trailers can't: city streets, jobsites, retail centers, residential areas
  • Exclusive use: your freight is the only freight, loaded once and unloaded once
  • Faster: straight to the consignee, no terminal stops
