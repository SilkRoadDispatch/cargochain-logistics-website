About CargoChain

One kind of truck. Done right.

CargoChain Logistics LLC has hauled interstate general freight for more than three years. We chose to specialize in 24–26ft straight box trucks, the owner-operators who run them, and the shippers who need them.

Our story

Why box trucks

CargoChain Logistics LLC is based in West Chester, Ohio, just north of Cincinnati. For more than three years we've hauled interstate general freight. Today five trucks run under our authority, based in Cincinnati, Chicago, Detroit, Birmingham & Orlando.

Box truck owner-operators are some of the hardest-working people in freight, and they're often the least supported. Most dispatch services treat them as an afterthought next to 53' trailers. We built CargoChain the other way around: our dispatch, rate knowledge, broker relationships and shipper programs are all built around the 26ft box.

Specializing makes us better for shippers and brokers too. We know what fits, what a liftgate can lift and which loads belong in a box truck.

Owner-operators first

When our owner-operators earn well and stay, everything else works: service, safety and reliability.

Straight talk

Clear settlements, honest ETAs and no hidden deductions. We say what we'll do, then do it.

Relationships over transactions
