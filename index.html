What does the 12% cover?

Lease-on to our authority, dispatch, load planning, rate negotiation, billing, and compliance support. You focus on the truck and the delivery; our team handles the back-office work.

02

How do I check my pay?

Weekly settlement statements show every load, rate, and deduction. Insurance is billed weekly based on your actual premium. Send signed BOL/POD paperwork and freight photos within 12 hours of delivery to help avoid payment delays.

03

Will the work fit my truck—and my life?

Start with your home base, preferred lanes, and home-time needs. Tell us your box length, liftgate setup, and truck details so we can discuss the fit. Lane availability and earnings vary.
