FAQ

Driver questions

Do I really not need a CDL?
Correct, for trucks with a GVWR of 26,000 lbs or less, which covers most 26ft box trucks. Federal rules require a CDL only above 26,000 lbs. You do need a DOT medical card for interstate driving.
Is this local or over-the-road?
Mostly regional and OTR interstate runs out of Cincinnati, Chicago, Detroit, Birmingham & Orlando. We match you to routes that fit your home-time needs.
Will I have to load and unload?
Some loads need driver assist using the liftgate and pallet jack. The load details tell you ahead of time.
