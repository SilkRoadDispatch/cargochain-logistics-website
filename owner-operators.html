FAQ

Owner-operator questions

Do I need a CDL to drive a 26ft box truck?
No, as long as the truck's GVWR is 26,000 lbs or less, which covers most 26ft box trucks. Check the sticker on the driver's door frame. Over 26,000 lbs you need a Class B CDL. Either way, interstate driving requires a DOT medical card.
Do I need my own MC authority?
No. You lease on and run under CargoChain's authority (USDOT 4081213, MC 1552696) and our insurance policy.
Do I need IFTA or IRP?
Usually not. IFTA and IRP generally apply to vehicles over 26,000 lbs or with 3+ axles. Most 2-axle 26ft box trucks at 26,000 lbs GVWR or less are exempt. We confirm this for your specific truck during onboarding.
How and when do I get paid?
Weekly, by direct deposit, with a settlement statement that shows every load, rate and deduction. Want it faster? Same-day pay is available through factoring at 2.5% of the invoice. Send your signed BOL/POD and freight photos within 12 hours of delivery so nothing holds up your pay.
Can I choose my lanes and home time?
Yes. Tell us where you want to run and when you want to be home, and your dispatcher plans around it. You approve every load before it's booked. There is no forced dispatch.
How does insurance work?
You run under CargoChain's policy: $1,000,000 auto liability and $100,000 cargo. Coverage usually costs $1,500–2,000 a month, depending on your truck and record. We deduct it weekly based on your actual premium, usually about $410 a week.
Is there a bonus for safe driving?
Yes. A clean DOT Level 1 inspection earns you up to $500.
Who pays for fuel and maintenance?
As an owner-operator you cover your truck's fuel, maintenance and payment. Your share of the gross is set to account for that. Our fuel card gets you discounts at the pump.
What kind of freight will I haul?
Interstate dry general freight: palletized retail goods, e-commerce, trade show materials, furniture and appliances, building supplies and more. It's a mix of spot-market loads and recurring lanes from our direct shippers and partner brokers.
Is there a contract or a buy-in?
No buy-in. The lease runs 12 months and renews automatically, and either side can end it with 14 days' notice.
